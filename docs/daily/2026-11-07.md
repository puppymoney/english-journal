# My Saturday & Beauty Salon

2026 年 11 月 7 日 · 每日英语口语复盘

> 本篇根据「英语口语练习」中已有的复盘摘要整理，作为网站首篇示例；并非完整逐字转写。英文跟读稿是整理后的练习文本。

## 1. 今日话题

周末生活、美容院与日常安排。重点是讲述已经发生的事情，并把中文习惯表达换成更自然的英语。

## 2. 原句与地道表达

| 复盘中记录的原句 | 更自然的表达 | 记住这一点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I get up late this morning. | **I woke up late this morning.** | 早上已经过去，使用一般过去时。 |
| I have a brunch. | **I had brunch.** | brunch 通常不用冠词 a；过去时用 had。 |
| Once in a month. | **Once a month.** | 表示频率时直接说 once a month。 |
| Make my eyes looks brighter. | **Make my eyes look brighter.** | make + 宾语 + 动词原形。 |

## 3. 今日核心词汇

| 表达 | 中文 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| beauty salon | 美容院 | I went to a beauty salon. |
| get my lashes done | 做美睫 | I went there to get my lashes done. |
| natural-looking lashes | 自然款睫毛 | I prefer natural-looking lashes. |
| look more awake | 看起来更精神 | They make my eyes look more awake. |
| once a month | 每月一次 | I usually go there once a month. |

## 4. 今日语法重点

**讲述过去：** 用 `woke up`、`had`、`went` 连接今天已经发生的事。

**使役结构：** `make + 宾语 + 动词原形`。

> The lashes make my eyes **look** brighter.

## 5. 英文跟读稿

以下是根据已有复盘内容整理的精简练习稿：

I woke up late this morning because it was Saturday. After brunch, I went to a beauty salon to get my lashes done. I usually go there about once a month.

I prefer short, natural-looking lashes. I don't wear much makeup, so I just want my eyes to look brighter and more awake.

### 跟读节奏

- I **woke up** / late this morning / because it was **Saturday**.
- I **usually** go there / about **once a month**.
- I prefer / short, **natural-looking lashes**.

先慢速读清楚，再按意群连起来。重点练习 `woke up` 的连读，以及 `usually` 的发音。
